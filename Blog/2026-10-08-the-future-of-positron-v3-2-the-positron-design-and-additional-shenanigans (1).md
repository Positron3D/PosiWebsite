Hello everyone,

This is an overall update on what we’ve been up to as a design group.

Over the last year, we’ve found it frustrating that we had no good way to communicate openly with the community. That’s why we’ve added this blog. It matters even more because many of our projects move slowly. We’re a small team, and progress takes time.

With that, here’s what we’re currently working on:

Current Projects:

Positron V3.2 toolhead refresh and Positron V3.3

Positron V3.9: a test bed Positron design packed with features we’d like to see on a Positron V4

Positron Power Board: a power switching board for the Positron design, and more

Prusa Mini Nextruder: adapting a Nextruder to the Prusa Mini, in collaboration with Prusa

Strangelove Board: a drop-in Klipper replacement for the Nextruder Loveboard

What About Prusawire?

Prusawire isn’t in active development after the R1 release, but we’ll continue to support it going forward.

What About Proton?

Proton currently on the backburner, we plan on finishing it at some point, however this will be after our current projects.

What’s Next

We’ll be posting more detailed blogs on each of these projects in the coming weeks. We apologize for the lack of updates, but rest assured, we are doing things!
