# Prusa brand rules

Source: **Prusa Research Brand Manual 1.0** and the official media kits at <https://www.prusa3d.com/page/media-assets_987/>. The manual on its own: `https://backend.prusa3d.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Prusa_brand_manual.zip`. The Prusa Research logo kit (`logo-kit/{rgb,cmyk}/{black,white}/`) is inside `https://prusa3d.com/downloads/press/prusaresearch.zip`.

## Logo (§1.1)

- **Black or white only.** The approved pairings are black on white, white on black, and **white on Prusa Orange**. The manual's contrast guide switches to the black logo only when the orange is lightened to about 50%.
- Safe zone: the width of the "P" in PRUSA. Minimum width: 80px (digital), 25mm (print).
- Don't deform, rotate, outline, add effects, rearrange, or place it on an inappropriate background.

## Colours (§1.2)

| Colour | HEX | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Prusa Orange | `#fd5000` | RGB 253/80/0, PANTONE Orange 21C. The current orange (since mid-2024). |
| Old orange | `#ed6b21` | Only for legacy assets that already use it. |
| Prusa Pro Green | `#00C48D` | The Prusa Pro line. |
| Grey | `#808285` | 60% black |
